# 빛의 정원 · Garden of Light

An original **virtual**, desk-scale light installation: three quiet apricot,
mist-teal and dusty-violet filters on a warm paper display slab. The light moves
through one small, continuous orbit. There is no flashing, sound, or camera cut.

## Files
- `Garden-of-Light.blend` — editable scene, materials, camera, and keyed lighting.
- `Garden-of-Light.png` — 800 × 800 still.
- `Garden-of-Light-loop.mp4` — 480 × 480, 48 frames / 12 fps, four-second loop.
- `build.py` — portable procedural source; all geometry and textures are original.
- `validation_*.json` — numerical checks from the build.

## Render again
Requires Blender 4.3+ and ffmpeg for video encoding. No downloads or external
assets are required. Outputs go next to the source, independent of working directory.

```sh
blender -b -t 2 --python build.py -- --render still
blender -b -t 2 --python build.py -- --render loop
ffmpeg -framerate 12 -i frames/frame_%04d.png -c:v libx264 -crf 19 \
  -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart Garden-of-Light-loop.mp4
```

The still uses Cycles CPU, two threads and 64 samples; the loop uses 24 samples.
Denoising is deliberately disabled because the tested Blender build does not
include OpenImageDenoise. Some fine-grained Monte Carlo noise is expected.
The loop encodes frames 1–48, not the duplicate endpoint at frame 49.

## What the light model means
These are **thin, non-refracting optical filter approximations**, not solid glass.
Their material combines colored transparent transmission and a small diffuse
surface contribution. Cycles traces the resulting colored light attenuation onto
the matte slab. The metal outlines and ceramic supports are ordinary shaded
geometry. This is not a spectral simulation, caustic-focusing simulation,
physical fabrication plan, or promise of real-world material performance.
The paper texture is procedural noise feeding subtle surface bump.

The scene's single area light changes position smoothly at constant power;
all panels, camera, and geometry remain stationary. No compositor-painted
shadows, projected color decals, animated exposure, or hidden colored lights.
