Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: an original standalone painting, not concept art for a game or a product.
Primary request: A restrained, large-scale painterly view of an empty indoor swimming pool after all the water has drained. The painting should make a viewer feel the weight and depth of the missing water without illustrating a metaphor literally.
Scene and composition: landscape format. View from low inside the deep end, with the off-white tiled floor filling most of the foreground and rising toward a distant shallow end. Asymmetrical, strongly cropped architecture, one high strip of windows near the upper right edge, dark quiet blue-grey walls. Two dark navy lane lines run away across the floor and curve up the far wall; their imperfect interrupted paint gives scale. A broad, pale warm rectangle of late-afternoon window light lies diagonally across the floor. One thin irregular puddle at the margin of that light reflects a narrow piece of the windows. The dry tile and the reflected window must remain distinctly different surfaces. No water filling the pool.
Style and materials: a deliberate gouache and dry-brush painting on subtly textured heavyweight paper; broad matte planes, very sparing scraped details, slightly uneven hand-painted edges, cool mineral blue-grey, chalk white, one quiet ochre light shape. Spatially convincing but visibly painted, not a photograph, not a 3D render, not an isometric miniature. High control of edges: the closest tile joints visible only intermittently, most distant joints lost in broad tone. No grain filter pasted over everything.
Mood: austere, still, awake, neither cute nor sentimental. Let the unoccupied space carry the picture.
Constraints: no people, no floating objects, no furniture, no plants, no signs, no legible text, no title, no frame, no watermark. Avoid a central symmetrical composition, saturated cyan, dramatic god rays, a fantastical or ruined building, and generic glossy digital concept-art lighting.
