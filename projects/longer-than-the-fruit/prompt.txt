Original painterly still life, landscape. Near-overhead intimate view of muted blue-green kitchen tabletop. Small freshly peeled ivory apple upper right third, faint uneven blush at stem hollow. Its one continuous long thin red skin peel lies loose, beginning near apple, broad irregular open loop towards left, one gentle crossing near bottom, small curled taper. Vermilion/wine-red outer skin and pale moist cut edge change orientation naturally around bends. Clearly delicate curling apple peel, not fabric, snake or thick spiral tube; surprisingly long beside compact fruit. Generous irregular empty tabletop inside loop. Assured opaque gouache and dry colored-pencil marks on subtly textured paper; flat tactile planes, visible brush decisions, no photorealistic 3D. Restrained mature palette, warm ivory fruit, narrow dark grounding shadows, diffuse upper-left afternoon light. Close asymmetric framing. No knife, hands, plates, other props, lettering, border, signature, watermark. Quiet observational painting whose uneven peel line and negative space carry composition.
